The resolved design is to replace the cargo's `RouteSpecification` through `Cargo.specifyNewRoute(...)`, preserving origin, destination, and itinerary, and then persist through `CargoRepository.store(...)`. ## Implement

Add the application-layer deadline-change use case, with no facade or web-layer changes:

- Add `void changeDeadline(TrackingId trackingId, Date deadline)` to `src/main/java/org/eclipse/cargotracker/application/BookingService.java`.
- Implement it in `src/main/java/org/eclipse/cargotracker/application/internal/DefaultBookingService.java`.
- Load with `cargoRepository.find(trackingId)`.
- Read the current destination from `cargo.getRouteSpecification().getDestination()`.
- Construct a replacement `RouteSpecification` from `cargo.getOrigin()`, that current destination, and the supplied deadline.
- Apply it with `cargo.specifyNewRoute(routeSpecification)`.
- Persist with `cargoRepository.store(cargo)`.
- Log the tracking ID and new deadline at `Level.INFO`, matching `changeDestination(...)` style.

Write the test first in `src/test/java/org/eclipse/cargotracker/application/BookingServiceTest.java`. Append sequential `testChangeDeadline()` after `testChangeDestination()`. Advance the original deadline by one month, invoke the service, reload through `Cargo.findByTrackingId`, and prove:

- origin remains Chicago and destination remains Helsinki;
- the stored deadline is the same calendar day as requested;
- the assigned itinerary is unchanged;
- transport status is `NOT_RECEIVED`;
- last known location is `Location.UNKNOWN`;
- current voyage is `Voyage.NONE`;
- the cargo is not misdirected and is not unloaded at destination;
- ETA is `Delivery.ETA_UNKOWN`;
- next expected activity is `Delivery.NO_ACTIVITY`;
- routing status remains `MISROUTED`.

Date handling must continue to use `java.util.Date`; do not introduce a timezone policy. The administrative UI will ultimately require a non-null date but no new chronological policy: no requirement that the replacement be after today, the old deadline, or itinerary legs.
